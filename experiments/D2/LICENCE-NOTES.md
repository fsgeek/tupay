# Licence notes for D2's sources

- **OSF DCE 1.2.2** (`file/episode/`, read from the public mirror github.com/nealelliott/dce_1.2.2 at `745a220`): released by The Open Group under the GNU LGPL in January 2005. The mirror's files still carry the earlier OSF (1995-96) and Open Group (1997) notices, not the LGPL text; the release is attested by Tony Mason, a DCE source licensee (log, 2026-11-03). Excerpts quoted in D2's committed files are from that release.
- **The Episode File System**, USENIX Winter 1992 (public copy https://fsgeek.ca/media/2017/11/1992-The-Episode-File-System.pdf): USENIX is open access ("free to publish, free to read"); quotations in D2's files are for scholarly analysis, with this citation.
- Neither the source tree nor the paper is committed here, and full author transcripts are kept outside the repository (hashes committed), as the design decided. That choice now rests on need, not licence: nothing tupay does requires them public.
